# v0.88.0 — Search and Grounding Recall: Design

Status: **draft for review** (2026-10-15; §2.1 and decision 1 revised the same day after the gate simulation). Not approved, not implemented. Renumbered from v0.87.0 on 2026-10-20 (v0.87 plan decision 12), when the handoff/registry milestone took v0.87.0; the findings below are unchanged and still need re-checking against v0.87.0 before approval. Live evaluation 75.x of v0.85.0 runs first; its results may add to or change this scope.

## 1. Why

Live evaluations 73.x and 74.x found seven search and grounding defects. Each was recorded for "a later search/grounding milestone" (`KNOWN_LIMITATIONS.md`, v0.85.0 paragraph; v0.84 plan §11.5 finding 4-6; v0.85 plan §4). One of them is not in the safe direction:

| # | Finding | Seen in | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | "how do I compute the 95th percentile of a column" grounds **full** on `setColDateFormats` | v0.84 §2.5, 74.15 | **Unsafe direction.** On `ask()`, generation is allowed and grounded on an unrelated page. On `gauss_search_docs`, the client is shown that page as the answer. |
| 2 | "percentile" resolves to nothing | 73.13, 74.15 | The `quantile` page is never offered. |
| 3 | "sort a matrix by a column" is ambiguous among `sortmc`, `sortr`, … and never offers `sortc` | 73.12, 74.14 | `clarify`, without the function that does it. |
| 4 | `for` is a question stopword, so the `for` page is unreachable; `endfor` resolves to nothing | 74.x §11.5 finding 5 | A search for `for` returns nothing at all. |
| 5 | A hyphenated candidate (`tspdlib-landing`) cannot be opened by searching its name | 74.x finding 5 | Breaks v0.83.0's contract: "to read a candidate, search its name". |
| 6 | Long-page excerpts stop before the Outputs section | 74.16, 74.18 | The output structure members (`out.par`, `out.fct`, `out.retcode`) come from prior knowledge, not the docs. |
| 7 | `command_doc_lookup.lookup` truncates to `max_results` before its tie check | v0.85 §2.2 | v0.85.0 fixed the validation path. Grounding's alias, derived-index and recent-topic paths still use `max_results=1`. |

Goals:
- no question grounds on a page through keyword overlap alone: grounding, a generation-gate input, rests on categorical evidence;
- the concept a 73.x/74.x question named reaches its documented function, through the existing curated-alias discipline;
- every page `gauss_search_docs` lists as a candidate can be opened by searching its name;
- an excerpt keeps its Outputs section;
- every per-symbol re-lookup in grounding reads the defining page.

**Unlike v0.83.0–v0.85.0, this milestone deliberately changes the `ask()` generation gate.** Findings 1–4 are all gate inputs. §6 therefore replaces "gate equivalence" with a **gate diff**: every changed `(status, policy, matched, unmatched, ambiguous)` tuple is listed and explained, and every row that moves toward generation (`block`/`clarify` → `supported_only`/`full`) is reviewed individually.

Constraints:
- no change to `command_doc_lookup.py` (`score_file`, `lookup`, `extract_match`) or to signature/type extraction (decision 6);
- the readiness state machine, `compute_eligibility`, `compute_evidence_profile`, every validator rule and the system-statement registry are unchanged, so no `validate_code` verdict changes except through finding 7 (§6.3);
- `compute_generation_gate` itself is unchanged. Only its inputs change.
- Everything stays deterministic and stdlib-only: fixed word lists, curated doc-verified aliases, exact file-name checks. No model and no score.

Out of scope:
- `keyword`/`fn` definitions read as undocumented calls (v0.85 §9.2 item 6);
- matrix literals with `.` elements treated as expressions (v0.85 §9.2 item 5);
- expression category inference (72.3 finding 1);
- `&` parameters (v0.83.0's decision);
- public release readiness.

## 2. Investigation findings

All probes ran on 2026-10-15 at `d634b40` (v0.85.0 + status), against the published docs copy (`C:/Users/Owner/.gauss-ai/docs`, 1,826 pages, 1,845 derived-index entries). The scratchpad scripts are not committed; their inputs and results are recorded here.

### 2.1 Finding 1: derived keyword overlap grounds unrelated pages

`resolve_concepts` runs the documentation-derived index (`gauss_doc_concept_index.resolve_derived_concepts`) once over the whole question. A page is proposed when its keywords share `MIN_KEYWORD_OVERLAP` (2) words with the question:
- page keywords come from the heading and Purpose text, minus `_GENERIC_DOC_WORDS` and any word in more than 5% of pages;
- **the question's words are not filtered at all.**

`setcoldateformats.rst`'s Purpose begins "Set **how** … each **column** …", so its keywords are `setcoldateformat, how, display, date, bsd, strftime, specifier, also, convert, column`. "how do I compute the 95th percentile of a column" shares `how` and `column`, so this is the only derived match, and it resolves `full`. `percentile`, the only real topic word, matches no page (§2.2).

English function words survive into page keywords across the corpus because they are too rare to reach the 5% filter. In entries: `use` 79, `one` 58, `are` 48, `two` 42, `all` 40, `get` 31, `these` 26, `not` 20, `its` 19, `more` 18, `new` 17, `only` 16, `any` 15, `has` 13, `out`/`than` 10, `but`/`such` 9, `also`/`same` 7, `those` 5, `other` 4, `then`/`most` 3, `was`/`there`/`some` 2, and `how`/`what` 1 each.

**A function-word list does not fix it; simulated.** A fixed list of 36 English function words (`how`, `what`, `the`, `are`, `use`, `one`, …) was added to `_GENERIC_DOC_WORDS` in memory, the index was rebuilt, and `ground_multi` + `compute_generation_gate` were compared before and after. The question set was every benchmark prompt's first paragraph plus v0.85's 61 gate questions. 20 gate tuples changed:
- the two 74.15 percentile questions went `full` (on `setColDateFormats`) → `block`, as intended;
- nine more false groundings disappeared, among them `vartypef`, `fclearerr`, `spOnes`, `robustSE`, `fn`, `schtoc`, `ismiss`, and `setColDateFormats` in "reshape … column means";
- **but four questions moved toward generation onto an unrelated page.** "The user asks … run" went `supported_only` → `full`, newly grounded on `getlrv` (`run`, `user`), and three others gained `lib`, `gdaPack`, `shell` or `rolling`.

The mechanism: `resolve_concepts` subtracts symbols the question already names, *after* picking the top score. At HEAD the top derived match for those questions is the `run` page itself, which is subtracted, leaving nothing. With `use`/`one` gone from `run`'s keywords, it drops to a 2-word tie with `getlrv`; `run` is subtracted again, and the junk match resolves.

**The real cause is the precision of the derived pass.** Measured directly (index + overlap only, no lookups) over 756 question-like inputs: the benchmark prompts, v0.85's gate questions and the 73.x/74.x search queries.
- **At HEAD it resolves 140 to a single page and reports 316 as ambiguous.** Ambiguity becomes `clarify` on `ask()`.
- **About 15 of the 140 are plausibly the page asked about:**
  - `meanc` for "column means" (5; the alias index already covers these);
  - `chol` for "Cholesky decomposition" (2);
  - one each: `logisticRegFit`, `zandrews`, `median`, `matrix-division`, the `rndseed` page, `packr`, `impute`, and the Python-users guide.
- **The rest overlap on ordinary words:**
  - `getlrv` on `run`+`user` (22×), `trace` on `execution`+`user` (13×), `plotSave` on `file`+`user` (7×);
  - `covmmt` on `label`+`print` ("print it with a label like det = …");
  - `xlsMakeRange` on `build`+`column`+`row`, `ssHeteroskedasticityTest` on `first`+`square`, `robustSE` on `small`+`use`;
  - `setColDateFormats` on `column`+`how`.
- Many inputs describe tester scenarios rather than GAUSS questions, which inflates the junk count. But the real GAUSS questions above show the same failure.
- **Variants don't help:**
  - with the function-word list: 142 resolved, 286 ambiguous;
  - with `MIN_KEYWORD_OVERLAP` = 3: 36 resolved, 32 ambiguous, and still mostly wrong (`msym` on `missing`+`use`+`user`, `plotSave` on `created`+`file`+`user`, `includedir` on `directory`+`path`+`working`).

Keyword overlap is a score, and CLAUDE.md asks that grounding, a generation gate input, rest on categorical evidence. Today a 2-word overlap can allow generation on an unrelated page.

### 2.2 Findings 2 and 3: the docs do not say "percentile" or "sort … by a column" where the index looks

- **"percentile".** 19 pages contain the word, but never in a heading or Purpose text. In `quantile.rst` it appears only in example output ("95 percentiles") and in a `.. seealso:: … :func:`percentile`` link to a page that does not exist. `quantile`'s documented inputs are "quantile levels or probabilities" (`e`). The derived index cannot reach `quantile` from "percentile", and no stemming rule should invent one.
- **"sort a matrix by a column".** The derived index ties `sortmc` (`sort, multiple, column`) and `sortr`/`sortrc` (`sort, column, …`) at 2, so the question is `clarify`. `sortc`'s Purpose is "Sorts a matrix, dataframe or string array.", with no "column", so its keywords are `sortc, sort, dataframe`. Its `:param c:` says "specifies the column(s) of *x* to sort on", but parameter text is not indexed.
- **A curated alias alone is not enough for finding 3.** In `resolve_concepts`, the derived pass subtracts symbols already resolved but still reports a tie among the rest. With a `sortc` alias, "sort a matrix by a column" would resolve `sortc` and still be `clarify` (§3.2).

### 2.3 Finding 4: stopwords that are page names

`_STOPWORDS` (87 words) drops `for` before any lookup, so a search for `for` has no tokens at all: `ungrounded/block`, nothing unmatched. Eight 3+-letter stopwords are also page stems: `between`, `code`, `comparison`, `create`, `error`, `for`, `show` and `use`. They are stopwords for good reason: in multi-word questions ("write code **for** the column means", "**use** meanc") they are filler.

`endfor` is not a stopword, but there is no `endfor.rst`. `for.rst`'s Format documents `for i (start, stop, step); … endfor;`, and `lookup("endfor")` finds no confident match.

### 2.4 Finding 5: hyphenated page names

`_TOKEN_RE` (`[A-Za-z][A-Za-z0-9_]{2,}`) splits `tspdlib-landing` into `tspdlib` and `landing`, and neither resolves. The page is `tspdlib/docs/tspdlib-landing.rst`, which `lookup("tspdlib-landing", ...)` finds by exact file name. 168 published pages have a hyphen in their stem, mostly guides (`cc/data-cleaning.rst`, `bet/bet-examples.rst`, `address-operator.rst`). `search_candidates` can list any of them, but none can be opened by name.

### 2.5 Finding 6: excerpts lose Outputs

`_format_doc_match` concatenates heading, syntax, Inputs, Outputs, Examples and Notes, then cuts at `max_chars` (`MAX_GROUNDING_CHARS_BUNDLED` = 1,500 for every `ground_multi` and `validate_code` source). Of the 1,174 documented symbols whose page has an Outputs section, **30 lose it entirely**, because their Inputs run past 1,500 characters. Most are structure-based estimators and optimizers whose control structure is documented inline:

`QNewtonmt`, `arimaFit`, `autoregFit`, `bvarSvFit`, `bvarSvForecast`, `coint_ghansen`, `dstatmt`, `eqSolvemt`, `fgls`, `gdaLoad`, `glm`, `gmmFit`, `gmmFitIV`, `impute`, `kernelDensity`, `loadd`, `minimize`, `mnpFit`, `morpATEFit`, `ols`, `olsmt`, `pd_stationary`, `quantileFit`, `sbur_gls`, `sqpSolveMT`, `svarFit`, `svarIrfCompute`, `switchFit`, `varmaFit` and `waldTest`.

Their Outputs sections are 135–895 characters. The excerpt is also read by `validate_generated_code` (`documented_symbols` collects call-shaped names in source content, on the `ask()` path), so §6.2 includes a verdict diff.

### 2.6 Finding 7: the remaining `max_results=1` re-lookups

After v0.85.0, `gauss_conversation_engine.py` still calls `lookup(..., max_results=1)` at three sites:
- `_lookup_command`, used by legacy `ground()` and the recent-topic fallback;
- the curated-alias verification in `resolve_concepts`;
- the derived-index verification in `resolve_concepts`.

v0.85 §9.3 measured all 1,418 documented symbols: `_lookup_defining_page` changes the page only for `miss`. Neither the alias index nor any expected derived match resolves to `miss`, so routing these sites through `_lookup_defining_page` is expected to change nothing measurable. It closes the class rather than a live defect. No other module calls `lookup` with `max_results=1`.

## 3. Proposed design

Deterministic and stdlib-only. No new module and no `pyproject.toml` change.

### 3.1 Derived-index matches become candidates, not grounding (finding 1)

**Proposed (decision 1, option A).** `resolve_concepts` stops turning documentation-derived keyword overlap into a `RESOLVED` or `AMBIGUOUS` resolution. Derived matches are listed instead as labeled `candidates`, through v0.83.0's `search_candidates` path (`gauss_search_docs` only). That path already labels each candidate, attaches its Purpose sentence, re-verifies it, and is structurally kept out of `GroundingBundle` and the generation gate.

Grounding then rests only on categorical evidence:
- a literal symbol;
- a curated, doc-verified alias;
- an error code;
- a recent topic.

The concepts the derived pass got right on real questions get curated aliases where the evidence supports one. Candidates from the §2.1 measurement: "Cholesky decomposition" → `chol`, "logistic regression" → `logisticRegFit`, "Zivot-Andrews" → `zandrews`, "least squares solution" → the `matrix-division` page, and "column medians" → `median`. Each is checked against its page before inclusion, and the final list comes from the §6.2 gate diff.

Effects:
- questions that ground today only through derived overlap become `ungrounded`/`partially_grounded`, so `ask()` blocks or generates only for the supported topics. **This is the safe direction**, and it is how `ask()` already treats any unmatched topic;
- the 316 derived ties stop forcing `clarify`. They appear as candidates on `gauss_search_docs`, and on `ask()` the question is gated by its other evidence;
- the `getlrv`-style subtraction artifact (§2.1) disappears with the pass.

**Rejected options:**
- **(B) A function-word list** (`how`, `what`, `the`, `are`, `use`, `one`, …) added to `_GENERIC_DOC_WORDS`. It fixes the 74.15 row, but it barely changes precision (140 → 142 resolved) and creates new false groundings in the unsafe direction (§2.1);
- **(C) `MIN_KEYWORD_OVERLAP` = 3.** It cuts resolutions to 36, still mostly wrong, and also drops the few right ones.

### 3.2 Two curated aliases, and an alias supersedes the derived pass (findings 2, 3)

Two `ConceptAliasEntry` rows, each held to the existing discipline: real `.rst` evidence, quoted, and re-verified against the caller's docs root at runtime:

| Phrases | Symbol | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| `percentile`, `percentiles` | `quantile` | `quantile.rst`: `:param e: quantile levels or probabilities.`; its example prints "95 percentiles" for `e = { .025, .5, .975 }` |
| `sort by a column`, `sort a matrix by a column`, `sort by column`, `sort on a column`, `sort rows by a column` | `sortc` | `sortc.rst`: `:param c: … specifies the column(s) of *x* to sort on.` |

**Rule change in `resolve_concepts`, needed only if decision 1 keeps derived grounding:** when a curated alias resolved, the derived whole-question pass is skipped. Today the derived pass can still turn an alias-resolved question into `clarify` (§2.2). Under decision 1 (A), the derived pass no longer resolves anything, so this rule is moot.

### 3.3 A one-word query is looked up even if it is a stopword (finding 4)

In `_candidate_tokens`, when the question is exactly one token (after stripping whitespace and a trailing `?`), that token is a candidate even if it is in `_STOPWORDS`. Multi-word questions are unchanged. This makes `for`, `use`, `show`, `create`, `error`, `between`, `code` and `comparison` searchable by name, and nothing else changes.

`endfor` gets a curated alias to `for` (evidence: `for.rst` Format, `for i (start, stop, step); … endfor;`).

### 3.4 Hyphenated page names (finding 5)

In `_candidate_tokens`, a hyphenated word (`[A-Za-z][A-Za-z0-9_]*(?:-[A-Za-z0-9_]+)+`) is a candidate token **only when a page with exactly that stem exists** under the docs root (the same exact-file-name check `lookup` itself performs). It is then resolved exactly like any literal token. Its parts are not also looked up. A hyphenated word with no such page is split as today.

### 3.5 Outputs survive the excerpt budget (finding 6)

In `_format_doc_match`, when the assembled text exceeds `max_chars` and the Outputs section would be cut, the Inputs section is shortened first, ending in `…`, so that heading, syntax and Outputs fit. Pages whose text already fits, or whose Outputs already fit, are byte-identical. Expected to change exactly the 30 pages in §2.5 at 1,500 characters, plus any page that loses Outputs at the legacy 4,000. The section order is unchanged.

### 3.6 Every grounding re-lookup reads the defining page (finding 7)

The three remaining sites use v0.85.0's `_lookup_defining_page`, each keeping its own status and confidence checks. `command_doc_lookup.lookup` is not changed (decision 6).

### 3.7 What does not change

- `command_doc_lookup.py` in full, and every extraction function.
- `compute_generation_gate`, `GroundingBundle`, `search_candidates`' ranking and labels, `MIN_KEYWORD_OVERLAP`, and `MAX_DOC_FREQUENCY_FRACTION`.
- Every validator rule, the system-statement registry, the readiness state machine, `compute_eligibility` and `compute_evidence_profile`.
- The 87-word `_STOPWORDS` for multi-word questions.

## 4. Scope boundary

In scope: §3.1–§3.6.

Out of scope:
- indexing parameter text for the derived index (it would fix finding 3 generally, but changes every entry's keywords);
- stemming or synonyms;
- a `tspdlib`-style package landing alias (the hyphenated page opens by name; the bare package name still does not);
- the §1 out-of-scope items.

## 5. Known limitations this design accepts

- **Recall still depends on curated aliases for words the docs never put in a heading or Purpose.** §3.2 fixes the two observed questions, not the class.
- **Under decision 1 (A), recall for plain-language concepts drops to the curated aliases.** A question naming no symbol and matching no alias is `ungrounded`: `ask()` blocks it, and `gauss_search_docs` lists candidates. This is the safe direction, but some correct derived groundings measured in §2.1 are lost unless they become aliases.
- A one-word query for a stopword page grounds `full` on that page (for example `show` → `show.rst`). This is the same behavior as any exact symbol name.
- A shortened Inputs section can drop parameter descriptions late in a long list. The full page is one search away, and Inputs are cut only to keep Outputs.

## 7. Live evaluation 79.x

After implementation:
1. Run the full suite.
2. Stop any `gauss-ai-mcp.exe`, then reinstall.
3. Reload the window.

Then the user asks plain-language **search-heavy** tasks in a fresh conversation. Useful shapes:
- concept questions phrased the way 73.x/74.x phrased them ("95th percentile", "sort by a column", loops);
- opening a listed candidate by name;
- an estimator or optimizer whose output members the client must use.

Record per `CLAUDE.md`, including whether the client followed a candidate back to a grounded page.

## 8. Decisions needed

1. **Derived keyword overlap stops grounding** (§3.1). Options:
   - (A) derived matches become labeled search candidates only, and the few concepts it got right get curated aliases;
   - (B) a function-word list;
   - (C) a 3-word minimum overlap.

   *Recommend (A).* (B) and (C) were measured in §2.1: each still grounds generation on unrelated pages, and (B) creates new false groundings. (A) is the larger gate change, and it is entirely in the safe direction: generation is only ever blocked or narrowed. It is the one decision here that changes v0.74.0's design, so it deserves an explicit choice.
2. **An alias supersedes the derived pass for the same question.** *Recommend yes* if decision 1 keeps derived grounding (B or C); otherwise moot. Without it, the `sortc` alias still leaves 74.14's question `clarify`.
3. **Stopword pages: the one-word rule plus an `endfor` alias.** *Recommend yes.* Removing `for` from `_STOPWORDS` instead would ground every "… for …" question on `for.rst`.
4. **Hyphenated words only when an exact page exists.** *Recommend yes.*
5. **Excerpts shorten Inputs to keep Outputs, and change nothing else.** *Recommend yes.* Reordering sections (Outputs before Inputs) would change every excerpt; raising the 1,500 cap grows every prompt.
6. **Finding 7 at the callers, `lookup` unchanged.** *Recommend yes.* Changing `lookup`'s truncate-then-tie order would change `status` for every `max_results=1` caller and break the "no `command_doc_lookup.py` change" invariant that every verdict diff since v0.82.0 relies on.
7. **Run 75.x before approving this plan**, and fold any 75.x search findings in. *Recommend yes.*
