## Decisions

- `call_argument_separator` was removed entirely rather than narrowed, once real evidence contradicted its structural-only justification — matches this milestone's own "prefer measurable disagreement over intuition-driven rule expansion" discipline.
- Two classes of remaining "false positive" are deliberately NOT treated as defects: (1) `matrix_literal_row_separator`'s intentional strictness (real GAUSS's process-level "acceptance" masks a dangerous silent misparse), and (2) `B-062`'s real-GAUSS-oracle limitation (GAUSS's own error-message selection can mask a genuine syntax defect depending on incidental symbol-table state). Both are documented in `docs/differential-syntax-validation.md` rather than papered over or used to justify weakening a rule.
- The grounding-token-cap fix adds a one-time-per-`docs_root`, `functools.lru_cache`d filename-only scan (no file content read) as a fourth priority tier — a deliberate, bounded, disclosed design choice, not a broader retrieval rewrite; `MAX_TOKEN_LOOKUPS` itself is unchanged.
- All other `v0.77.0` design decisions are recorded in `planning/v0.77.0-differential-syntax-validation.md` — that file is the durable record.
