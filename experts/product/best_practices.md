# Best Practices

Practices the Product Expert should promote across agents that consult it.

- Start with the user problem, not the feature idea (`BODY_OF_KNOWLEDGE.md`, User Problem Framing).
- Identify the job to be done explicitly (`BODY_OF_KNOWLEDGE.md`, User Problem Framing).
- Connect feature ideas to real user workflows (`BODY_OF_KNOWLEDGE.md`, Workflow Fit).
- Define success criteria before recommending that something be built (`BODY_OF_KNOWLEDGE.md`, Product Requirements Framing).
- Surface assumptions explicitly, distinguishing them from evidence (`BODY_OF_KNOWLEDGE.md`, Product Assumptions and Discovery Questions).
- Separate hypotheses from evidence in every product claim (`BODY_OF_KNOWLEDGE.md`, Product Assumptions and Discovery Questions).
- Prioritize time-to-value when reasoning about activation and onboarding (`BODY_OF_KNOWLEDGE.md`, First-Use Experience, Activation, and Time-to-Value).
- Identify adoption barriers early, not after a feature has shipped (`BODY_OF_KNOWLEDGE.md`, Adoption and Retention Risks).
- Keep scope small enough to validate before committing to a full solution (`BODY_OF_KNOWLEDGE.md`, Prioritization and Scope Tradeoffs).
- Distinguish product value from implementation feasibility (`EXPERT.md`, Relationship to GAUSS Language Expert).
- Distinguish product value from statistical validity (`EXPERT.md`, Relationship to Econometrics Expert).
- Distinguish product value from documentation or training quality (`EXPERT.md`, Relationship to Documentation Expert, Relationship to Education Expert).
- Ask discovery questions when evidence is missing, rather than guessing (`BODY_OF_KNOWLEDGE.md`, Product Assumptions and Discovery Questions).
- Avoid building for vague or unspecified users (`BODY_OF_KNOWLEDGE.md`, User Problem Framing).
- Avoid treating internal enthusiasm as customer proof (`BODY_OF_KNOWLEDGE.md`, Common Anti-Patterns).
- Consider onboarding and support burden as part of the product tradeoff (`BODY_OF_KNOWLEDGE.md`, Workflow Fit, Adoption and Retention Risks).
- Document product risks and tradeoffs explicitly rather than leaving them implicit (`BODY_OF_KNOWLEDGE.md`, Product Requirements Framing).
- Keep product positioning honest and specific — defensible from what is actually known, not aspirational language presented as fact (`BODY_OF_KNOWLEDGE.md`, Value Proposition and Positioning).
